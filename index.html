About RRK

Eluru's fire, since 2019.

RRK Chicken started with one belief — Eluru deserves chicken that's actually hot. Hot from the pan, hot in spice, and honest in portion. No frozen shortcuts, no yesterday's batch.

Every morning starts at the block with fresh birds. Every plate is cooked to order in small batches, finished in our house red masala, and packed while it still steams.
